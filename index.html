Build a 3D model from photos

Place your photos as cameras around the object, draw and triangulate its shape, and check it against every photo.

  1. Import photos of one object from several sides.
  2. Position the cameras: Arrange, gizmo, Align or Solve from pins.
  3. Model with points, lines and faces — or triangulate from two photos.
  4. Check any photo overlaid on the model, then export.

Tip: drag & drop photos, a saved project or a point cloud anywhere.
